# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Xbox 360 emulator users on Windows, Linux, macOS, and Steam Deck. The approved
portal design targets controller use in Steam Deck Gaming Mode, with keyboard,
mouse, and touch access to the same operations.

## Product Purpose

Manage a local Skylanders figure library and portal slots inside Xenia. Keep
figure saves recoverable and show capability limits before users start a game.

## Brand Personality

Practical, direct, familiar. Follow Xenia's existing menus and overlay controls.

## Anti-references

Avoid separate desktop tools, pointer-only actions, hover-only instructions,
retail artwork, invented figure names, and unsupported compatibility claims.

## Design Principles

- Use the existing gamepad dialog and input-blocking lifecycle.
- Give every operation a visible label and predictable focus order.
- Keep save errors visible until the user resolves or dismisses them.
- Require explicit approval before overwriting an export.
- Show support limits independently of successful library management.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The approved design requires A/Enter activation, B/Escape back, full-row
selection, and controls at least 44 logical pixels high. Selection and errors
must remain clear without color. No WCAG certification is claimed.
